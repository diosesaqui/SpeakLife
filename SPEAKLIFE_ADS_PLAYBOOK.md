## 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

**The App:** SpeakLife: Renew Your Mind is a 4.9⭐ Christian faith app offering Bible affirmations, audio devotionals, and guided prayer (3–5 min sessions). Built for Christians seeking daily encouragement, peace over anxiety, and Scripture-rooted mind renewal.

**The Business Model:** Freemium → Subscription. $10/month or $50/year with a **3-day free trial** on the annual plan.

**The Revenue Goals:**
- **Phase 1:** $10K MRR (~1,304 active subscribers)
- **Phase 2:** $100K MRR (~13,040 active subscribers)

### The Core Strategy in One Paragraph

Run Facebook **App Promotion** campaigns optimized for the **StartTrial** event. Build four campaigns: cold prospecting, lookalikes, post-install retargeting, and trial winback. Spend $40K–$50K total to reach $10K MRR, scaling from $50/day (testing) to $1,000/day (scaling). Lead with the **3-day free trial** in every ad — never the price. Win with UGC-style video ads that open with a pain hook (anxiety, spiritual dryness), show app value, prove it with 4.9⭐ social proof, and close with "Start your FREE 3-day trial." Target Christian app users (YouVersion, Hallow, Glorify) first, then expand to communicators like Joyce Meyer, Joel Osteen, and Elevation Church. Keep churn below 7% — every 1% reduction saves $2–5K/year in ad spend.

### Non-Negotiables
- ✅ **ALWAYS** lead with the free trial — never the $50 price
- ✅ **ALWAYS** reframe $50/year as "less than $1/week"
- ✅ **NEVER** use "Subscribe Now" as a CTA
- ✅ **NEVER** guilt-trip or use vague spiritual language
- ✅ **NEVER** scale budget more than 20% per 7-day period

## 3. PHASE 1 PLAYBOOK: $0 → $10K MRR

**Total Investment Required: ~$40,000–$50,000**
**Timeline: 7–12 months (depending on creative performance and churn)**

---

### 📅 MONTHS 1–3: TESTING PHASE
**Budget: $50–$100/day | Total Spend: ~$6,750**

**Goal:** Find what works before spending big money. Validate creative, audiences, and funnel.

#### What To Do:
- [ ] Install Facebook SDK and map all 5 events (see Section 7)
- [ ] Verify **StartTrial** event fires correctly (after Apple payment, not button tap)
- [ ] Launch **2 campaigns only:** TOF Prospecting CBO + Retarget ABO
- [ ] Test 3–5 ad creatives per ad set (UGC-style video priority)
- [ ] Target Tier 1 interests only (YouVersion, Hallow, Glorify, Bible.com, Abide)
- [ ] Optimize for **AppInstall** in first 2 weeks (to gather data), then switch to **StartTrial**
- [ ] Build your **custom audiences** from day 1 (installers, video viewers) — you'll need them at scale

#### Success Metrics for Month 3:
- CPA for StartTrial: under $50 (room to optimize)
- At least 1 creative with >3% CTR
- StartTrial event firing reliably
- iOS 14+ event sharing configured
- 50+ installs collected for retargeting

#### Key Actions:
1. Start with Broad + Tier 1 Interests in separate ad sets
2. Run 15–30s video ads with 3 different hooks (pain, aspiration, curiosity)
3. Kill ad sets spending >$50 without a trial conversion
4. Document which hooks stop the scroll (3s video hold rate >40% = winner)

---

### 📅 MONTHS 4–6: OPTIMIZATION PHASE
**Budget: $200–$400/day | Total Spend: ~$27,000**

**Goal:** Find your repeatable cost-per-trial, build your lookalike seeds, double down on winners.

#### What To Do:
- [ ] Launch all 4 campaigns (add Lookalike CBO + Winback ABO)
- [ ] Build 1% Lookalike from: (a) annual subscribers, (b) StartTrial audience (180-day)
- [ ] Scale winning creatives: duplicate winning ad sets with 20% budget bump
- [ ] A/B test landing experience: direct App Store vs. in-app preview
- [ ] Add Tier 2 interests (Joyce Meyer, Joel Osteen, Steven Furtick, TD Jakes)
- [ ] Set up 48-hour post-install retargeting (highest ROI window)
- [ ] Launch trial winback: target "started trial, no purchase" within 3-day window

#### Success Metrics for Month 6:
- CPA for StartTrial: $30–$45
- Creative rotation: 3+ proven winners
- MRR: $2,000–$4,000
- Monthly churn: below 10% (ideally 7%)
- Lookalike audiences seeded and active

#### Key Scaling Rule:
> **Never increase any campaign budget more than 20% in any 7-day period.** Bigger jumps reset the algorithm's learning phase and spike CPA.

---

### 📅 MONTHS 7–12: SCALING PHASE
**Budget: $600–$1,000/day | Total Spend: ~$99K–$185K**

**Goal:** Flood winning campaigns with budget, diversify audiences, and push toward $10K MRR.

#### What To Do:
- [ ] Scale TOF CBO and Lookalike CBO aggressively (still 20% rule)
- [ ] Expand to Tier 3 interests (Hillsong, Elevation Worship, K-LOVE Radio, The Bible Project)
- [ ] Test Tier 4 wellness crossover (anxiety, Calm app, morning routine)
- [ ] Launch Advantage+ App Campaigns alongside manual structure (compare CPAs)
- [ ] Refresh creative every 4–6 weeks — 3 new UGC pieces minimum per cycle
- [ ] Segment by gender: ~65% female audience — test female-only ad sets
- [ ] Test demographic splits: 25–34 vs. 35–44 vs. 45–54
- [ ] Start building retention programs (push notifications, in-app streaks) to combat churn

#### Success Metrics at $10K MRR:
- CPA consistently <$30
- LTV:CAC ratio >2.5:1
- Monthly churn <7%
- 3+ proven creative winners in active rotation
- $10K MRR sustained for 4+ consecutive weeks

## 4. CAMPAIGN ARCHITECTURE

### The 4-Campaign Structure

| # | Campaign Name | Type | Budget Split | Optimization Event | Notes |
|---|--------------|------|-------------|-------------------|-------|
| 1 | TOF Prospecting | CBO | 40% of total | StartTrial | Broad + Interest targeting |
| 2 | Lookalike Audiences | CBO | 30% of total | StartTrial | LAL seeds from subscribers |
| 3 | Retarget: Installed, No Trial | ABO | 15% of total | StartTrial | Manual control needed |
| 4 | Winback: Trial, No Purchase | ABO | 5% of total | Subscribe | Urgent messaging |

> **Why CBO for Campaigns 1 & 2?** CBO lets Facebook find the best ad sets dynamically — ideal for broad testing at scale. Min $75/day for CBO to exit learning phase.
> 
> **Why ABO for Campaigns 3 & 4?** Retargeting audiences are small. ABO gives you manual budget control so small audiences don't get starved. CBO would dump all budget into the largest ad set.

---

### Campaign 1: TOF Prospecting CBO
- **Objective:** App Promotion
- **Budget:** 40% of daily spend (CBO, min $75/day)
- **Optimization:** StartTrial (after 50+ events; use AppInstall first if new)
- **Ad Sets:**
  - Broad (US, no interest targeting) — let Advantage+ audiences find buyers
  - Tier 1 Christian Apps (YouVersion, Hallow, Glorify, Bible.com, Abide, Pray.com)
  - Tier 2 Communicators (Joyce Meyer, Joel Osteen, Steven Furtick, TD Jakes)
- **Creative:** UGC-style video, pain + aspiration hooks, 3-day trial CTA

---

### Campaign 2: Lookalike Audiences CBO
- **Objective:** App Promotion
- **Budget:** 30% of daily spend (CBO, min $75/day)
- **Optimization:** StartTrial
- **Ad Sets (in priority order):**
  1. 1% LAL from Annual Subscribers (LTV-weighted — best seed)
  2. 1% LAL from StartTrial Custom Audience (180 days)
  3. 1% LAL from 75%+ Video Viewers (devotional content)
  4. 1% LAL from App Installers (30 days)
- **Note:** Start with #1 and #2. Build #3 and #4 as audiences grow.

---

### Campaign 3: Retarget — Installed, No Trial (ABO)
- **Objective:** App Promotion
- **Budget:** 15% of daily spend (ABO — manual per ad set)
- **Optimization:** StartTrial
- **Ad Sets:**
  - Installed 0–48 hours, no trial started → **URGENT messaging** ("You downloaded — ready to try it free?")
  - Installed 3–14 days, no trial started → **Social proof messaging** ("Here's why 1,100+ people love it")
- **Key insight:** 48-hour post-install window = highest ROI of any audience segment. Prioritize budget here.

---

### Campaign 4: Winback — Trial, No Purchase (ABO)
- **Objective:** App Promotion
- **Budget:** 5% of daily spend (ABO)
- **Optimization:** Subscribe
- **Ad Set:**
  - Started trial, no subscription (within 3-day trial window)
  - Messaging: Remove final objection. Show value. Remind them what they'd lose. No guilt — just clarity.
  - "Your 3-day trial ends soon. Less than $1/week to keep the peace going."

### The Golden Rule of Speaklife Creative
> **Lead with the 3-day FREE trial. Always. The price ($50/year) is NEVER the hook — the transformation is.**

---

### Video Ad Structure (15–30 seconds)

```
[0–3 seconds]   HOOK — Pain point or aspiration. Stop the scroll.
[3–10 seconds]  VALUE — Show the app. Audio devotionals. Scripture declarations.
[10–20 seconds] PROOF — 4.9⭐ rating. User testimonials. Real results.
[20–30 seconds] CTA  — "Start your FREE 3-day trial"
```

#### Example Script (Pain Hook):
> **[0–3s]** "Do you start every morning anxious before you even open your Bible?"
> **[3–10s]** *[Screen recording: opening app, Scripture affirmation playing, peaceful music]* "Speaklife gives you 3-minute audio devotionals that speak God's Word over you — every single morning."
> **[10–20s]** "4.9 stars. Over 1,100 people say it changed how they talk to themselves."
> **[20–30s]** "Start your FREE 3-day trial today. No risk. Just peace."

---

### UGC Creator Brief

**Format:** Selfie-style video, casual setting (bedroom, kitchen, car)
**Length:** 20–45 seconds
**Tone:** Authentic, personal, not polished

**Script Framework:**
> "I used to struggle with [anxiety/feeling distant from God/starting my day scattered]...
> I'd try to read my Bible but my mind was everywhere. Then I found Speaklife.
> Every morning now, I open the app and it just... speaks Scripture over me. Straight into my ears.
> [Optional: show the app briefly]
> It's 3 minutes. That's it. And it changed how I talk to myself all day.
> Honest? The 4.9 stars don't surprise me anymore. Try it free for 3 days — you'll see."

**Creator direction:** No teleprompter feel. Stumbles are okay. Real > polished.

## 7. SDK & TRACKING SETUP

### Facebook SDK Event Priority

| Priority | Event Name | When It Fires | Why It Matters |
|----------|-----------|--------------|----------------|
| 🥇 1 | **Subscribe** | After subscription confirmed | The money event — ultimate optimization goal |
| 🥈 2 | **StartTrial** | After Apple payment confirmation ⚠️ | Primary campaign optimization event |
| 🥉 3 | **CompleteRegistration** | After account created | Mid-funnel signal for lookalike building |
| 4 | **AppInstall** | After first app open | Volume event — use only in launch phase |
| 5 | **ViewContent** | After viewing devotional/content | Top-of-funnel signal |

> ⚠️ **CRITICAL:** `StartTrial` must fire ONLY after Apple's payment sheet is confirmed — NOT when the user taps the "Start Trial" button. Premature firing corrupts your optimization signal and inflates reported trials. Verify this in Events Manager.

---

### iOS 14+ Configuration Checklist

- [ ] Verify domain in Business Manager (speaklife.app or relevant domain)
- [ ] Configure **Aggregated Event Measurement** in Events Manager
- [ ] Set event priority order: Subscribe > StartTrial > CompleteRegistration > AppInstall > ViewContent
- [ ] Enable **SKAdNetwork** (SKAN) attribution
- [ ] Use **App Promotion** objective only (required for iOS app campaigns)
- [ ] Wait for 50 StartTrial events before switching optimization from AppInstall → StartTrial

---

### Event Verification Steps
1. Install **Meta Events Manager** app or use Events Manager web interface
2. Trigger each event manually on a test device
3. Confirm events appear in real-time in Events Manager
4. Specifically test: tap "Start Trial" button → Apple payment sheet → confirm payment → verify StartTrial fires AFTER confirmation, not before
5. Cross-reference with Apple App Store Connect analytics weekly

## 8. PHASE 2 TRIGGER CHECKLIST

**DO NOT begin Phase 2 ($10K → $100K MRR) until ALL of the following are true:**

### The 5 Gates to Phase 2

| Gate | Requirement | Why |
|------|------------|-----|
| ✅ **Revenue Gate** | $10K MRR for **4+ consecutive weeks** | One good week isn't a business. Prove sustainability. |
| ✅ **Efficiency Gate** | CPA consistently <$30 | Unit economics must be proven at scale, not just hoped for |
| ✅ **Retention Gate** | Monthly churn <7% | At $100K MRR, 10% churn = $10K/month bleeding out |
| ✅ **LTV Gate** | LTV:CAC >2.5:1 confirmed by 6-month cohort data | Real data, not projections. Early cohorts must prove it. |
| ✅ **Creative Gate** | 3+ proven creative winners in active rotation | One creative dies and you need 2 more. Never scale on 1 winner. |

### Phase 2 Requires
- Dedicated in-house creative production (or agency retainer)
- Customer success / retention function (not just ads)
- Probably a dedicated media buyer or growth lead
- Expansion beyond US (CA, UK, AU, NZ — all strong English-speaking Christian markets)
- Advantage+ campaign testing alongside manual structure
- Android app (opens Meta's full Android audience)

### Daily Checks (Takes 10 Minutes)

1. Any ad set spending without trials? → Pause if $50+ spent, 0 trials
2. Any creative with 3s hold rate dropping below 30%? → Flag for refresh
3. Any audience frequency >4? → Expand or rotate creative
4. Total spend on pace with daily budget? → Check delivery issues

### Weekly Review (Takes 30 Minutes)

1. Winner/loser analysis by ad set — kill bottom 20%
2. CPA trend — up 3 weeks in a row = refresh creative or audience
3. Budget pacing — are you on track for monthly spend target?
4. Creative refresh needed? (>4 weeks old = test something new)

### Monthly Review (Takes 2–3 Hours)

1. Cohort analysis: what's the real 30-day trial → subscribe rate?
2. Churn check by cohort: Month 1 churn vs. Month 3 churn
3. LTV calculation update: is real LTV tracking toward projection?
4. Budget increase planning: where to add next 20%?
5. Phase 2 gate check: are any gates now passable?
