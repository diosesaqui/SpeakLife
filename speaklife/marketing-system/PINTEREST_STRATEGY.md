## Pin Format (What Performs Best on Pinterest)

**Size:** 1000 x 1500px (2:3 ratio — tall pins get more reach)
**Text on image:** Large, readable, 1-2 lines max
**Brand elements:** Navy (#1a2744) or gold (#c9a84c) accents, consistent font

**3 pin types to rotate:**

### Type A — Declaration Card
- Dark navy or teal background
- Large declaration text in white
- Scripture reference in gold
- Small SpeakLife logo bottom right
- Example: "I am not anxious. God holds my future." — Phil 4:6-7

### Type B — Question Hook
- Warm background (cream, soft gold)
- Question at top: "Struggling with anxiety this morning?"
- Answer below: "Speak this over yourself..."
- Short declaration
- "Download SpeakLife — free on iOS" at bottom

### Type C — List Pin
- "5 Declarations to Speak Over Your Day"
- "3 Scriptures for When You're Overwhelmed"
- "Morning Routine for Christian Women"
- These get saved heavily and drive traffic for months

## Posting Schedule

**Minimum:** 5 pins per day (can batch-schedule with Tailwind or Pinterest's own scheduler)
**Optimal:** 10–15 pins per day (mix of new + repins from other faith accounts)
**Best times:** 8–11pm EST (Pinterest traffic peaks at night when women are winding down)

### Week 1 — Launch
- Set up all 7 boards
- Create 20 declaration graphics (one per day for 20 days)
- Pin 5x per day from day 1

### Week 2-4 — Build
- Add list pins (5 declarations for anxiety, morning routine, etc.)
- Start following and repinning from @BibleVerseDaily, @WomenInTheWord, other faith accounts
- Link AEO pages from pins (best-christian-affirmation-app, christian-app-for-anxiety, etc.)

### Month 2+ — Compound
- Pinterest SEO compounds — pins from month 1 will still be getting discovered
- Double down on whatever board gets the most traffic
- Add seasonal content (New Year declarations, Easter, back to school)
