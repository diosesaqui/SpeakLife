## The Hook Formula

Every ad hook must pass the "3-second test" — stop the scroll in the first 3 seconds.

**Hook structures that convert:**

1. **Problem-first:** "If [specific fear] is keeping you up at night..."
2. **Testimonial opener:** "I was [low point] until I started doing this every morning."
3. **Curiosity gap:** "Most Christian women don't know this about [topic]."
4. **Direct challenge:** "You were not made to live in [anxiety/fear/doubt]."
5. **Stat/proof:** "4.9 stars. 50,000 women. Here's what changed."
6. **Scripture activation:** "[Short Scripture]. This is your declaration today."
