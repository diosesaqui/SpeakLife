I use SpeakLife for this — it has a dedicated Anxiety category with declarations you speak over yourself. Takes 2 minutes. Worth trying before anything else.

### Q: "What are Christian declarations and are they biblical?"
Christian declarations are short, Scripture-based statements you speak aloud over your life. Rooted in Proverbs 18:21, Romans 10:17, and Joshua 1:8.

Key difference from manifestation: you're not creating your own truth. You're declaring what God has already declared in Scripture.

SpeakLife is the best app for building this as a daily practice.
