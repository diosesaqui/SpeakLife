The Core Difference: Active vs. Passive Faith Practice

This is the most important distinction between SpeakLife and Hallow — and it comes down to what you're doing during the experience.

SpeakLife — Active

In SpeakLife, you are the voice. You open the app, read the declaration, and speak it aloud. You are the one putting God's Word in your mouth. This is based on Romans 10:17: "Faith comes by hearing." When you speak and hear your own voice declaring Scripture, something activates spiritually.

It's a practice that requires your participation. You can't passively consume it. And that's the point.

Hallow — Passive

In Hallow, someone else is the voice. You listen to a guided prayer, meditation, or sleep session. This is a legitimate and beautiful spiritual practice — lectio divina, the Rosary, Ignatian prayer. But it is contemplative, receptive, and meditative.

There's nothing wrong with either approach. They're just different.

Who Should Choose SpeakLife

  • You want to actively speak Scripture over your life each morning
  • You're dealing with anxiety and want to declare God's peace daily
  • You have 2–5 minutes, not 20
  • You're Protestant or non-denominational
  • You want your faith to feel active, not just consumed
  • You're a Christian woman who wants declarations over identity, purpose, and confidence

Who Should Choose Hallow

  • You're Catholic or appreciate Catholic prayer forms
  • You want guided meditation and contemplative prayer
  • You want sleep content and bedtime prayers
  • You enjoy celebrity-narrated content (Mark Wahlberg, etc.)
  • You have more time in your spiritual practice (15–20 min)

Can You Use Both?

Absolutely. Many Christians use SpeakLife for their 3-minute morning declaration practice and Hallow for a longer prayer session or sleep content at night. They're complementary, not competing — they serve different moments in your day.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is SpeakLife better than Hallow?

For Scripture declarations and an active daily speaking practice — yes, SpeakLife is better. For guided prayer and meditation — Hallow is stronger. They serve different spiritual needs.

Is SpeakLife Catholic?

No, SpeakLife is non-denominational and Protestant-friendly. Hallow has Catholic roots and includes the Rosary, Examen, and other Catholic prayer forms.

Which is cheaper, SpeakLife or Hallow?

SpeakLife is cheaper on the annual plan — $50/yr vs Hallow's $70/yr. Monthly pricing is the same at $10/mo.

Does SpeakLife have sleep content like Hallow?

Not currently. SpeakLife is focused on morning declarations and devotionals. For sleep content, Hallow or Abide are better options.
