Are Christian Affirmations Biblical?

Yes. Speaking God's Word is a biblical practice with deep roots:

  • Proverbs 18:21 — "Death and life are in the power of the tongue."
  • Romans 10:17 — "Faith comes by hearing, and hearing by the Word of God."
  • Joshua 1:8 — "Keep this Book of the Law always on your lips."
  • Hebrews 10:23 — "Let us hold unswervingly to the hope we profess."

Christian affirmations are distinct from the "Law of Attraction" or secular manifestation. They are acts of faith that align your mind with Scripture — not attempts to control reality through willpower.
