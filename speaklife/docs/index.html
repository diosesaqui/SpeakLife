Frequently Asked Questions

Is SpeakLife free to download?

Yes, SpeakLife is free to download on iOS. The app offers a 3-day free trial on the annual plan ($50/year) and a monthly subscription at $10/month.

What are Christian declarations?

Christian declarations are Scripture-based statements you speak aloud over your life. Based on Proverbs 18:21 ("death and life are in the power of the tongue"), declarations help align your mind and heart with what God says about you.

How is SpeakLife different from a Bible app?

Bible apps like YouVersion are great for reading Scripture passively. SpeakLife is active — you speak God's Word over specific areas of your life (anxiety, identity, confidence, etc.) in short, powerful declarations. It's less study, more activation.

Is SpeakLife for men or women?

SpeakLife is designed with Christian women in mind, but men use and love it too. The declarations are written to be personal and applicable regardless of gender.

How long does it take each day?

Most users spend 2–5 minutes with SpeakLife in the morning. It's designed to fit into a busy life — not require one.
