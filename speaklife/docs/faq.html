SpeakLife FAQ

Everything you need to know about the Christian declaration app.

About SpeakLife

What is SpeakLife?

SpeakLife is a Christian iOS app that helps you speak Scripture-based declarations daily. It delivers fresh declarations every morning across 6 categories: Anxiety, Joy, Destiny, Wisdom, Identity, and Confidence. The practice is built on the biblical principle that faith comes by hearing God's Word (Romans 10:17).

Who is SpeakLife designed for?

SpeakLife is designed with Christian women aged 25–40 in mind — women who want an active, Scripture-based morning practice that fits into a busy life. Men use and love the app too. If you want to speak God's Word over your life daily, SpeakLife is for you.

What are Christian declarations?

Christian declarations are short, Scripture-based statements that you speak aloud over your life. They're rooted in Proverbs 18:21 ("death and life are in the power of the tongue") and Romans 10:17 ("faith comes by hearing"). Speaking God's Word activates faith and replaces anxiety, doubt, and fear with biblical truth.

Are declarations biblical?

Yes. Speaking God's Word is a biblical practice anchored in Proverbs 18:21, Romans 10:17, Joshua 1:8, and Hebrews 10:23. Every declaration in SpeakLife is tied to a specific Scripture so you know exactly where it comes from.

Is speaking declarations the same as manifestation?

No. New Age manifestation says you create your own reality through thought and speech. Christian declarations agree with what God has already declared in Scripture. The authority is God's Word — not your belief in yourself. It is an act of faith, not an attempt to control reality.

Features & Usage

What categories does SpeakLife cover?

SpeakLife covers 6 categories: Anxiety (peace, trust, releasing worry), Joy (gratitude, contentment), Destiny (purpose, calling, direction), Wisdom (decisions, clarity), Identity (who God says you are), and Confidence (courage, boldness, strength).

How long does it take each day?

2–5 minutes. SpeakLife is designed to fit into a morning routine, not consume it. The declarations are short and clear so you can speak them confidently and quickly.

Does SpeakLife have audio?

Yes. SpeakLife includes audio devotionals you can listen to or speak along with. Perfect for commutes, morning walks, or any time you want guided audio.

Does SpeakLife send notifications?

Yes. SpeakLife includes daily push notification reminders to keep you consistent with your morning practice.

Comparisons

How is SpeakLife different from YouVersion?

YouVersion (Bible App) is built for reading Scripture passively through plans and daily verses. SpeakLife is built for speaking Scripture — active declarations over specific life areas. They complement each other well. Many people use YouVersion for study and SpeakLife for their morning declaration practice.

How is SpeakLife different from Hallow?

Hallow is Catholic-rooted and focused on guided prayer, meditation, and sleep content. SpeakLife is non-denominational and focused on active Scripture declarations. Hallow is passive (you listen); SpeakLife is active (you speak). SpeakLife is also $20/year cheaper on the annual plan. See the full comparison →

How is SpeakLife different from Abide?

Abide is primarily a meditation and sleep app with Christian themes. SpeakLife is primarily a declarations and devotional app for morning activation. Abide is passive; SpeakLife is active. Different use cases.
