## 1. Brand Overview

**Name:** SpeakLife: Renew Your Mind
**Developer:** DiosEsAqui LLC
**App Store tagline:** "Renew Your Mind"
**Core promise:** Activate what's already yours in Christ.
**Rating:** 4.9/5 · 1,100+ ratings · Free on App Store

### Voice Adjectives (5)
1. **Honest** — Speaks to real pain without flinching
2. **Grounded** — Scripture-backed, never hype or prosperity gospel
3. **Warm** — Friend who's been through it, not a pastor on a stage
4. **Direct** — Short sentences. Punchy. No corporate wellness energy
5. **Specific** — "Overthinking at 3AM" beats "anxiety"; emotionally precise

### Positioning
SpeakLife is the daily mind-renewal habit for Christian women who know their faith is real but feel the gap between what they believe and how they live. It closes that gap with audio-first declarations — truths you hear AND declare out loud, 3–5 minutes a day, in the language of your actual pain.

### Tone in One Sentence
> "God already handled this. Here's the verse that proves it."

### Competitive Differentiation

| Feature | SpeakLife | Glorify | Hallow | Abide |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Core mechanic** | Active declaration (speak out loud) | Devotionals + worship | Catholic prayer/meditation | Bible audio |
| **Format** | 3–5 min audio declarations | Daily devotional + livestream | Guided prayer, sleep content | Bible + narration |
| **Audience focus** | Women 25–40, real pain states | Broad evangelical | Catholic Christians | Bible readers |
| **Hook style** | Pain-first → Scripture → Declaration | Inspirational | Liturgical | Educational |
| **Daily habit driver** | Pillar rotation, audio habit | Streaks + community | Pray with others | Reading plans |

| **Time commitment** | 3–5 min (fits anywhere) | 10–30 min | 10–20 min | Variable |

**SpeakLife's unfair advantage:** Passive listening is easy to skip. Active declaration creates a physiological and psychological loop — when you speak a truth out loud, you encode it. No other faith app has built this as its primary mechanic.

## 2. Visual System

### Typography
- **Primary font:** Montserrat Bold — ALL CAPS for hooks and declarations
- **Secondary font:** Montserrat Regular / Light — for scripture references, supporting text
- **Hierarchy rule:** Hook = largest, Declaration = medium, Scripture = smallest, CTA = medium-bold

### Exact Brand Colors

| Role | Hex | Description |
|---|---|---|
| Background (primary) | `#0D0D1A` | Near-black deep navy — the signature SpeakLife dark |
| Background (alt) | `#111827` | Dark charcoal — secondary dark bg option |
| Text (primary) | `#FFFFFF` | Pure white — all main copy |
| Accent (gold) | `#C9A84C` | Warm gold — key words, highlights, CTAs |
| Accent (amber) | `#F5A623` | Bright amber — high-emphasis moments |
| Text (muted) | `#9CA3AF` | Gray — "the world says" contrast text |
| Danger/before | `#EF4444` | Red — "before" state, negative thoughts |
| CTA button | `#C9A84C` on `#0D0D1A` | Gold on dark |

### CTA Style
- Always: `"4.9★ · Free on the App Store"` — never omit the rating
- Button style: Gold text, no filled background, or subtle gold border
- Position: Bottom 15% of frame
- Never: "Download now", "Try today" — always use the App Store social proof format

## 3. Photography Direction (Static Ads)

### Lighting
- **Cinematic, low-key** — 70–80% of the frame stays dark; single source of light
- **Warm rim lighting** — gold/amber backlight on subjects (candle-warm, 2700K feel)
- **No flat studio lighting** — must feel atmospheric, not corporate
- **Light ray textures** — acceptable for scripture cards; subtle, not cheesy

### Mood
- **Premium faith app** — feels like a luxury wellness brand, but for the soul
- **Reverent without being religious** — no crosses as focal points, no stained glass
- **Emotionally honest** — a woman sitting in quiet, hands on Bible, morning light; not posed stock photo happiness
- **Cinematic darkness** — think Samsung brand meets Christian faith

### Composition
- **Portrait formats** (4:5, 9:16) dominate — mobile-first
- **Text-heavy layouts welcome** — SpeakLife's brand IS the words; let them breathe
- **Dark background with text** outperforms lifestyle photography (per LEARNINGS.md)
- **Gold keyword highlighting** — single most effective visual signal for saves and shares
- **Negative space** — especially around declarations; crowded = lower completion rate

### Subject Matter
- Christian women, 25–40, modest presentation (no cleavage, no revealing clothing — NON-NEGOTIABLE)
- Morning routines: coffee, Bible, phone, quiet
- Emotional honesty: hands covering face, eyes closed in prayer, sitting alone — NOT forced smiling
- Minimal props: journal, phone showing app, simple table setting
- Avoid: stock-photo "diversity collage", church buildings, crosses as main subject

## 5. Ad Creative Style

### Proven SpeakLife Formats (ranked by performance)

1. **IF YOU [PAIN STATE], DECLARE THESE** — most consistent performer; scroll-stopper
2. **Command-Pair:** STOP [PASSIVE] / START [ACTIVE] — no setup needed; divine-instruction feel
3. **Identity Contrast:** THE WORLD SAYS / GOD SAYS — dramatic contrast; high saves
4. **Social proof:** 4.9★ + review quote + app UI — trust-building, high CTR
5. **Google Symptom Hook:** Faux search UI — viral format, crossover reach
6. **Countdown Curiosity:** "THE 3rd DECLARATION CHANGES EVERYTHING" — completion-bait

### Text Overlay Approach
- Hook: ALL CAPS, Montserrat Bold, large, centered or left-aligned
- Declaration text: Title Case or ALL CAPS, gold for key words
- Scripture: Small, bottom of frame, light gray
- Gold highlighting: Single most effective visual signal — use for the "payoff word" in each declaration
- No more than 3 text levels visible at once (hook / declaration / scripture)

### Hook Patterns (top-performing)
```
IF YOU [HYPER-SPECIFIC PAIN STATE], DECLARE THESE
STOP [PASSIVE BEHAVIOR] — START [DECLARATION ACTION]
THE WORLD SAYS [LIE] — GOD SAYS [TRUTH]
I GOOGLED MY [SPIRITUAL SYMPTOMS] — GOD SAID THIS
THE [3rd] DECLARATION IS THE ONE THAT CHANGES EVERYTHING
IF YOU'VE EVER FELT [SPECIFIC EMOTION], THIS IS FOR YOU
YOUR [HEALING/BREAKTHROUGH] IS CLOSER THAN YOUR NEXT BREATH
DECLARE THIS BEFORE THE ENEMY DOES
```

### What Never Works
- Leading with the app name in the hook
- Churchy buzzwords: "blessed", "anointed", "breakthrough" (in hooks)
- "Are you struggling with…" — too generic
- White/bright backgrounds
- Generic "God loves you" without emotional specificity
- Walls of text appearing all at once
