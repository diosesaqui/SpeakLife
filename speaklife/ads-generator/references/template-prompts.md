## T01 — Declaration Hook Card

**Aspect ratio:** 4:5
**needs_product_images:** false
**Notes:** This is the scroll-stopper frame 1 — the entire ad lives or dies by the hook. No app UI, no clutter. Pure typographic punch. The pain state must be hyper-specific (e.g., "wake up anxious before your feet hit the floor" not "feel anxious").

### Prompt:
Static social media ad image, 4:5 portrait format. Deep dark near-black background (#0D0D1A) with a very faint atmospheric texture — subtle grain or smoke wisps barely visible, adds depth without distraction. No bright elements except the text.

Large ALL CAPS Montserrat Bold headline centered in the upper two-thirds of the frame, pure white (#FFFFFF), taking up approximately 60% of the image width. The headline reads: "IF YOU [PAIN STATE], DECLARE THESE" — the phrase [PAIN STATE] rendered in warm gold (#C9A84C) to visually separate it from the surrounding white text, creating immediate visual hierarchy and a focal draw to the pain point.

Below the headline, a thin horizontal gold accent line (#C9A84C), approximately 40% of frame width, centered, 2px thick. This is the only decorative element on the card.

At the very bottom center of the frame, small SpeakLife wordmark logo in white, approximately 8% of frame width. Below it, in very small Montserrat Regular text: "speaklife.app" in muted gray (#9CA3AF). No CTA button — this is the hook card only.

The overall mood: a divine instruction card. Feels like God wrote it. Premium, clean, cinematic dark energy. Montserrat Bold typography throughout. No stock imagery, no photographs, no people. Pure typographic design on atmospheric dark.
