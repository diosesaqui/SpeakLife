# SpeakLife UGC Playbook — What Makes Great UGC Ads

## The #1 Rule
The moment it looks like an ad, it stops working. Every decision must serve authenticity.

## The #2 Rule (added March 2026)
**Emotion first. Revelation second. Always.**
The hook is never about faith. The hook is about a feeling the viewer already has.
Faith is the answer — not the opening line.
If your hook contains "God", "Jesus", "scripture", "prayer", "faith", "Bible" → rewrite it.
The cold audience doesn't know they need SpeakLife yet. They know they're exhausted, anxious, empty.
Speak to that. Faith earns its reveal once they feel seen.

## The Anatomy of a High-Converting UGC Ad

### The Hook (0–3 seconds) — Everything
- 3 seconds is all you get. The hook is the entire game.
- **RULE: Zero faith language in the hook. Pure emotion, pure relatability.**
- **Best performing hook types for faith/wellness apps:**
  1. **Confession hook:** "I used to wake up every morning in complete panic..."
  2. **Contrast hook:** "I tried therapy, journaling, meditation... then I found this"
  3. **Direct address:** "If you can't turn your brain off at night, watch this whole thing"
  4. **Bold claim:** "This app literally changed how my brain starts the day"
  5. **Visual hook:** Holding phone showing the app open, mid-scroll
  6. **Exhale hook:** A visible sigh, exhale, or moment of stillness — then start speaking
- Hook must match the PAIN of the target viewer — not the product, not the faith
- First word matters: "I", "If", "When", "This" — not "Hey guys" or "So..."
- **Hook self-check:** Would a non-religious woman stop scrolling? If no → rewrite.

### The Body (3–20 seconds) — Build believability
- Tell the before story briefly: what life felt like with the problem
- Introduce the discovery naturally: "I came across this app..." / "My friend sent me this..."
- Show genuine emotion — not excitement, but RELIEF. Relief is the most powerful emotion for faith apps.
- Use natural language, filler words OK ("like", "honestly", "literally") — makes it real
- Never use corporate language. Never say "this product". Say "this app", "it", "this thing"

### The Proof (15–30 seconds) — Make it believable
- Show the app being used ON screen (screen recording or phone in hand)
- Specific details sell: "I open it every morning before I check Instagram"
- Quote a specific verse or declaration that hit them personally
- Show the emotional payoff — not "it worked!" but "I cried when I read this..."

### The CTA (last 3–5 seconds) — Soft sell only
- Never hard sell in UGC. It breaks the spell.
- Best CTAs: "It's free so I mean... just try it" / "Link is in bio if you want it" / "I just sent it to my sister"
- The CTA should sound like a recommendation, not a command

## Creator Archetypes That Crush for SpeakLife

### 1. The Relatable Struggler (highest converter)
- Regular woman, 25-35, no makeup or light makeup, casual setting (bedroom, car, kitchen)
- Tone: honest, slightly vulnerable, like texting a friend
- Scenario: Morning anxiety, relationship stress, feeling lost in life
- Hook: "I don't talk about this much but..."

### 2. The Transformed Believer
- Someone who found faith/renewed their faith through the app
- Tone: genuine joy, not hype — calm, grounded happiness
- Scenario: "I grew up Christian but kind of fell off... this brought me back"
- Hook: "I've been a Christian my whole life but I was still waking up terrified every day"

### 3. The Skeptic Converted
- "I'm not really into apps like this but..."
- Tone: slightly reluctant recommendation — more believable because of it
- Scenario: Friend recommended it, didn't expect much, now uses it daily
- Hook: "My friend kept sending me these verses and I finally downloaded the app she got them from"

## Visual & Technical Rules

### What HIGH QUALITY UGC looks like (not production quality — authentic quality)
- **Camera:** Front-facing phone camera, NOT professional camera. Slight hand movement = real.
- **Lighting:** Natural light from a window OR warm indoor lighting. NOT ring light (screams ad).
- **Background:** Real environment — bedroom, car, coffee shop, gym. NOT a plain wall.
- **Framing:** Slightly off-center or casual framing. NOT perfectly composed.
- **Clothes:** Casual, relatable. NOT a professional outfit or company-branded anything.
- **Editing:** Jump cuts OK. Subtitles always. No fancy transitions. Text overlays only if platform-native style.

### What KILLS UGC authenticity instantly
- Ring light (looks too produced)
- Mentioning the brand name in the first 3 seconds
- Reading from a script (eyes moving, stiff delivery)
- "Hey guys!" opening
- Overly perfect hair/makeup
- Showing a discount code
- Saying "use my link" in the first 10 seconds

## Script Structure (30-second format)

```
[0-3s]  HOOK — Pure pain/relatability. No product mention.
[3-12s] BEFORE STORY — What life felt like with the problem
[12-22s] DISCOVERY + PROOF — How you found it, what specifically resonated
[22-28s] RESULT — The emotional shift (relief, peace, clarity) — not features
[28-30s] SOFT CTA — "It's free, just try it" / "Link in bio"
```

## SpeakLife-Specific Script Templates

### Template 1: Morning Anxiety (highest volume pain point)
```
HOOK: "I used to check my phone the second I woke up and spend the next hour spiraling."
BEFORE: "My anxiety was there before I even got out of bed. I'd already decided the day was going to be bad."
DISCOVERY: "I started using this app called SpeakLife — it sends you Bible verses and declarations throughout the day. I was skeptical but I tried it."
PROOF: "The first morning, I got this verse about fear. And something just... shifted. Like God knew exactly what I needed."
RESULT: "I can't explain it. But I actually look forward to waking up now."
CTA: "It's free. Just try it for a week."
```

### Template 2: Identity / Not Enough
```
HOOK: "I could be in a room full of people and still feel completely invisible."
BEFORE: "Therapy, journaling, self-help books — nothing really stuck. I'd feel better for a day then right back."
DISCOVERY: "A girl I follow posted a verse about identity in Christ and I downloaded the app she mentioned."
PROOF: "There's a whole category just for identity. I go through it every morning now. Some of these declarations hit so different — like 'I am fearfully and wonderfully made' — I say it until I believe it."
RESULT: "I don't have it all together but something in my mind is genuinely different."
CTA: "It's called SpeakLife. It's free. I sent it to all my friends."
```

### Template 3: Skeptic/Lapsed Faith
```
HOOK: "I hadn't felt like myself in years. I just went through the motions every day."
BEFORE: "It wasn't that I stopped believing. I just stopped... practicing. Life got busy. I got anxious. I felt far from God."
DISCOVERY: "My mom kept sending me these little declarations. I finally asked where they were from."
PROOF: "It's this app — you pick a category, like Joy or Peace, and it just feeds you scripture throughout the day. It sounds simple but the consistency of it — morning, noon, night — it adds up."
RESULT: "I feel like I'm rebuilding something I thought I'd lost."
CTA: "It's SpeakLife. Free on the App Store."
```

## What the Copywriter Should Output Daily

2 UGC scripts per day, formatted as:
```
CATEGORY: [today's pillar]
ARCHETYPE: [Relatable Struggler / Transformed Believer / Skeptic Converted]
HOOK: [first 3 seconds — spoken word]
SCRIPT: [full 30-second script with timing notes]
VISUAL NOTES: [setting, wardrobe, what to show on screen]
CAPTION: [ready-to-post caption for the video]
```
