"He is like a tree planted by streams of water that yields its fruit in its season, and its leaf does not wither. In all that he does, he prospers." (Ps 1:3)

Look at the verse before it. The man who prospers is the one whose "delight is in the law of the LORD, and on His law he meditates day and night." (Ps 1:2) The prospering isn't random — it flows out of what he keeps his mind planted in.

That's the whole image. A tree by the water doesn't strain to produce fruit. It's just connected to the right source, so fruit is the natural result.

So don't shrink this promise down to one area of your life. "In all that he does" means all of it.

Meditate on this today, out loud:

Everything I put my hands to prospers.
My business and my career prosper — favor goes before me and wisdom guides my decisions.
My body prospers — life and health flow through me.
My family prospers — peace rules my home and God's promises cover everyone under my roof.
My mind prospers — I think God's thoughts and make God's choices.
I am planted by the water. My leaf does not wither. In all that I do, I prosper.

Say it until you believe it, then act like someone who does.

You're not hoping for fruit. You're planted. 🌳
