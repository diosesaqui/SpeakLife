# SpeakLife — Ad Creative Brief (Warfare & Outcomes Onboarding)

> For the designer. Everything here is pulled from the live onboarding flows so the ad creative **matches the landing experience** (message match = higher conversion). When a user taps an ad, the first onboarding screen should feel like the same world: same angle, same voice, same look.

---

## 1. What SpeakLife is

A faith app that turns Scripture into **spoken declarations**. The core mechanism: you don't just *read* the Word, you *speak* it over your life. Anchor verse used throughout: **"Death and life are in the power of the tongue." (Proverbs 18:21).**

We are testing **two onboarding angles**. Each ad should be built to feed **one** of them so the hand-off is seamless.

| Angle | Onboarding | Emotional lever | One-line promise |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Warfare** (fight-first) | thief → it's already yours → speak → activate | Fear of loss / take it back | "The enemy is stealing what's already yours. Take it back with your voice." |
| **Outcomes** (dream-first) | the won life, shown screen by screen | Aspiration / the after | "This is the life God already paid for. Speak it into reality." |

Both angles must do two things in every ad:
1. **Hit both triggers** — the *cost of staying stuck* AND the *dream outcome of acting now*.
2. **Make them feel something.** Love it or hate it, never neutral. Emotion → action → conversion.

---

## 2. The visual system (match the app exactly)

Pulled from the onboarding screens. Designer should treat this as the creative's base layer so the ad → app transition is invisible.

**Background**
- Full-bleed cinematic photo (mountains, sky, light through clouds, a person in a quiet/struggling moment).
- Dark gradient overlay, top-to-bottom: `black @ 82%` → `black @ 55%` → `black @ 72%`. Text always sits on the darker zones.

**Typography** (system rounded, i.e. SF Pro Rounded — designer can sub a geometric humanist rounded sans)
- **Eyebrow / kicker:** ~12pt, BOLD, UPPERCASE, letter-spacing ~1.4, white @ 50%. (e.g. `THERE'S A FIGHT OVER YOUR LIFE`)
- **Headline:** ~32pt, bold, white, centered, tight line breaks.
- **Body:** ~17pt, regular, white @ 75%, generous line spacing.
- **Scripture line:** ~15pt, **serif italic**, white @ 80%, in quotes.
- **Verse reference:** ~12pt, medium rounded, white @ 45%.

**Iconography**
- Single SF Symbol, 40pt, white, centered inside a 96pt circle filled `white @ 10%`. Examples in use: `exclamationmark.shield.fill` (threat), `checkmark.seal.fill` (paid for), `bolt.fill` (power/victory), `heart.fill` (health), `house.fill` (peace/home), `key.fill` (provision), `crown.fill` (identity), `hourglass` (the cost of waiting).

**Primary button**
- White capsule, black text, ~17pt semibold rounded, tall (56pt). Always action-forward with a `→`.

**Accent**
- Success green for checkmarks: `RGB(0.36, 0.84, 0.55)`. Use sparingly (proof/plan ticks only).

**Mood:** reverent but urgent. Cinematic, dark, hopeful light. Not cheesy "Christian stock." Think A24 trailer meets quiet devotional.

## 3. Warfare ad concepts (fight-first)

Source copy is verbatim from the warfare onboarding — reuse it so the ad and the first screen rhyme.

### Concept W1 — "The Thief" (cold open, strongest hook)
- **Hook (0–2s / headline):** "The thief came to take it all."
- **Build:** "Your health. Your provision. Your peace." (rapid cuts, each word on its own beat)
- **Turn:** "But it was already bought and paid for."
- **Payoff / CTA:** "Take it back with your voice." → *Download SpeakLife*
- **Visual:** dark, tense → light breaking in on the turn. Icon motif: shield → seal → bolt.
- **Scripture sting:** John 10:10.

### Concept W2 — "Stop Begging"
- **Hook:** "You're not begging God for what He's already given."
- **Line:** "You're an heir defending an inheritance."
- **CTA:** "Stand your ground. Speak." → *Start free*
- Best for an audience that feels spiritually worn out / striving.

### Concept W3 — "Read vs. Speak" (mechanism)
- **Hook:** "You've read it. Have you spoken it?"
- **Line:** "Reading calms the mind. Speaking takes ground."
- **Visual:** split screen — closed book (old way) vs. waveform/mouth speaking (SpeakLife).
- **CTA:** "Speak life." Scripture sting: Proverbs 18:21 / Romans 10:17.

**Warfare CTA bank:** *I'm Ready to Fight · Take It Back · Stand Your Ground · Speak Life*

## 4. Outcomes ad concepts (dream-first)

Source copy verbatim from the outcomes onboarding "vision" screens.

### Concept O1 — "How much longer will you wait?" (stakes → dream)
- **Hook:** "How much longer will you wait?"
- **Build:** "The healing. The open doors. The peace at home. They were never meant to be someday promises."
- **Turn to dream:** show the after (see vision lines below).
- **CTA:** "Show me what's possible." → *Download SpeakLife*
- **Visual:** `hourglass` icon, waiting/heavy → light, breath, relief.

## 5. Targeting → angle map (so ads route to the right onboarding)

The onboarding personalizes by "burden." Build ad sets around the same buckets and the creative will match the first screen perfectly.

- **Anxiety / overwhelm / can't sleep →** Peace (warfare or outcomes)
- **Health scare / chronic illness / caregiver →** Health
- **Money stress / job / provision →** Provision
- **Lost / shame / "who am I" →** Identity
- **Stuck / calling / purpose →** Purpose
- **Joyless / numb / going through motions →** Joy
- **"I'm not in crisis, I just want more" →** Warfare "all of it" / growth

## 6. Formats & specs

- **9:16** — Reels / Stories / TikTok / Shorts (primary). 1080×1920.
- **4:5** — Feed (IG/FB). 1080×1350.
- **1:1** — Feed fallback / carousel. 1080×1080.
- **Video length:** 15–30s for hook-tests; 6s bumper cutdowns from the best performers.
- **Safe zones:** keep text out of the top 250px / bottom 420px on 9:16 (UI chrome).
- **Caption-friendly:** assume sound-off. Burn in the hook text; declarations should be readable silently.
- **Variants per concept:** at least 3 hook treatments (different first 2 seconds) — that's where win/lose is decided.

---

## 7. Hard rules

- **No em dashes or en dashes** in any on-screen copy (brand rule). Use two short sentences.
- **First person where it's a declaration** ("I am healed"), second person where it's the promise to the viewer ("Your body, restored").
- **Scripture must be accurate** (NIV preferred) and paired with its reference.
- **Never neutral.** If a frame doesn't make someone feel threatened-then-rescued or longing-then-hopeful, cut it.
- **Match the app's restraint** — dark, cinematic, few words per frame. No clutter, no clip-art crosses, no loud gradients outside the spec.

---

## 8. Quick-start shot list (for the editor)

1. **Frame 1 (hook):** big headline on a dark cinematic plate. One line. Hold 1.5–2s.
2. **Frames 2–4 (stakes/build):** the cost — short, escalating lines, tight cuts, tension in the footage.
3. **Frame 5 (turn):** light breaks. The reframe ("already paid for" / "what's possible").
4. **Frames 6–7 (dream):** the after — the won life, the relief on a face, the calm home.
5. **Frame 8 (mechanism, optional):** speaking the Word (mouth/waveform), 1 beat.
6. **End card:** logo + white capsule CTA + app store badges. Scripture reference small.
