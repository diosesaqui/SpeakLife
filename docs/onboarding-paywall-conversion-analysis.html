1. Executive Summary

The good news: your funnel architecture is fundamentally correct. You have a Day-0 onboarding paywall (where 82–90% of all trial starts happen), hard gating in onboarding (hard paywalls convert ~5x freemium with no retention penalty), an annual-default plan selector with per-month anchoring and a SAVE% badge, real social proof, a personalized headline system, and a pay-what-you-can escape valve that doubles as App Review insurance. Most apps get this skeleton wrong; you didn't.

The gap is in the three highest-evidence patterns you haven't built yet, plus a layer of analytics bugs that are currently making it impossible to know what's working:

1 → 6–8
Quiz questions. Your flows ask one question before the paywall. Every top performer asks 15–30. Lose It! saw double-digit trial-rate gains just from lengthening; Adapty case: +40% conversions.
+10–15%

+23%
Trial starts from a Blinkist-style trial-timeline section ("Today → Day 2 reminder → Day 3 charge"). Now the Apple-safe successor to the banned trial toggle. Your paywall has one line of trial text.
+10–15% ARPU
From a decline path. Today paywall_dismissed fires and nothing happens. A single one-step exit offer (discounted annual or longer trial) is the cheapest revenue you're leaving on the table.

And the bad news that's actually urgent: your per-step onboarding events go to Firebase only (never PostHog), segment is empty on every paywall event for product/warfare users, weekly purchases are logged as "monthly", and trial_started can double-fire or fire for non-trial purchases. You cannot currently trust the funnel data you'd use to judge any of the improvements above. Instrumentation fixes should ship first.

The five patterns with the strongest documented evidence

  1. Long personalization quiz (15–30 purposeful screens). Sunk-cost investment + the "promise of personalization." Lose It!: double-digit trial-rate gains purely from adding questions. Web funnels: up to +60% vs direct-to-paywall. Bible Chat (the fastest-growing faith app, ~$15M ARR in year one) runs a 20-step quiz including denomination.
  2. "Building your plan…" processing screen + named plan reveal. FunnelFox: "ask a question, add a loader — that's an extra 10–15% conversion." Adapty 2026 calls a loader with social-proof checkmarks "table stakes."
  3. Trial-timeline ("honest") paywall. Blinkist: +23% trial starts, push opt-in 6%→74%, massive complaint reduction. RevenueCat explicitly names it the replacement for the trial toggle (rejected by Apple since Jan 2026).
  4. Decline-path offers. 24-hour welcome offer to non-converters or exit offer on dismissal: +10–15% ARPU (Superwall, Adapty, RevenueCat native feature since Dec 2025). Keep it one step deep (Guideline 5.6 risk).
  5. "No payment due now" reassurance + trial repetition. Cal AI's signature; restate the trial in headline, mid-page, and under the CTA.

3. What You Have Today (Audit)

3.1 The flows at a glance

WARFARE (11 screens):

Thief narrative→ Already paid for→ Speaking = weapon→ Activation→ Burden picker (1 question)→ First declaration taste→ Personal declaration (mandatory, no skip)→ Rating ask→ Product recap→ PAYWALL (hard)→ Notification time + OS prompt

PRODUCT (10 screens):

Storm hook→ 60-second speed→ Read vs Speak mechanism→ Product recap→ Burden picker (1 question)→ First declaration taste→ Personal declaration (mandatory, no skip)→ Rating ask→ PAYWALL (hard)→ Notification time + OS prompt

3.2 What's already strong KEEP

  • Day-0 hard onboarding paywall in every arm, with the showPayWhatYouCanLink Remote Config flag as a single kill-switch to soften it. Exactly right per the data.
  • The "first declaration taste" screen is your Headspace-breathing-exercise equivalent — a felt value moment, burden-matched, before the ask. This is genuinely best-practice and most apps in your niche don't have it.
  • The personal declaration step is a powerful commitment device ("I Spoke It. Send It to Me Every Day.") AND it feeds the warmest paywall headline ("Speak it daily until it comes to pass."). Research says commitment devices right before the paywall produce real (if modest) lifts — yours is stronger than Flo's thumb-hold because it's content, not theater.
  • Annual default + per-month anchoring + SAVE% badge, live StoreKit prices with a phantom-price guard, per-user Apple-verified trial eligibility. The OptimizedSubscriptionView fallback-price problem was correctly fixed here.
  • Pay-what-you-can is a faith-native generosity valve nobody else in the niche has framed this well ("God sees the heart") — and it satisfies 3.1.1.
  • Rating ask before the paywall (peak goodwill, pre-money-ask) matches what Cal AI and top H&F funnels do.
  • Notification permission last, after time selection, with a mock push preview of their actual declaration — textbook primer pattern.
  • Personalized headline waterfall (personal declaration → quiz segment → survey goal word → category fallback). Superwall: personalized paywalls beat generic by 15%+.

3.3 Where it diverges from best-in-class GAPS

  • Quiz depth: 1 question. Both flows ask only the burden picker. The narrative screens (thief/paid-for/weapon and hook/speed/mechanism) are tap-throughs — they build emotion but zero investment. Investment is what the data rewards.
  • No plan-building loader, no plan reveal. After the rating ask, users hit the paywall cold. There is no "Building your battle plan…" moment, no named plan ("Your 30-Day Peace Plan"), no projection. The paywall sells a subscription, not their plan.
  • No trial timeline on the paywall. Trial communication is one hardcoded line ("Free for 3 days. Cancel by day 3 to pay nothing.") — which also silently lies if Remote Config points at a 7-day-trial SKU.
  • No decline path. Dismissal is instrumented but unmonetized. No exit offer, no 24h welcome offer, no winback after trial cancellation. (The old discount-timer machinery in PremiumView is dead code.)
  • No "No payment due now" line under the CTA, and the trial is mentioned once, not repeated.
  • No early testimonial wall (Bible Chat opens with one before its quiz) — all social proof is concentrated on the paywall itself.
  • Personal declaration step has no skip — a mandatory voice/text input two screens before the paywall is your single biggest unmeasured drop-off risk (and you can't measure it, because its events don't reach PostHog).

5. HighConversionPaywall — What I Would Change

P0 5.1 Add a trial-timeline section above the plan selector

Insert a vertical 3-step timeline between the comparison grid and the sticky purchase block, rendered only when the user is trial-eligible, with the day numbers read from the actual StoreKit intro offer (fixes the hardcoded "3 days" bug at the same time):

  • Today — 🔓 "Full access unlocked. Speak your first declaration over your battle."
  • Day {n−1} — 🔔 "We'll remind you before your trial ends." (Only promise this if you send your own local reminder notification — Apple's is inconsistent, which your code comment already knows. Build the local reminder; it's also Blinkist's push-opt-in engine: 6%→74%.)
  • Day {n} — ⭐ "Your subscription starts. Cancel anytime before."

Expected impact: +15–23% trial starts (Blinkist case; RevenueCat-endorsed pattern)

Effort: ~1–2 days incl. reading intro-offer period from StoreKit and a local "trial ending" notification.

P0 5.2 Add the reassurance stack under the CTA

Directly beneath "Start Free Trial": ✓ No payment due now  ·  ✓ Cancel anytime in Settings — and restate the trial in the headline area ("Try everything free for {n} days"). Trial repetition (headline + mid-page + CTA) measurably reduces perceived risk. Keep the billed annual price the largest price element (Apple 3.1.2).

Expected impact: small but consistently positive; zero risk

Effort: hours.

P0 5.3 Build the decline path (one step deep)

On paywall_dismissed from onboarding, route to a single recovery screen instead of straight to advance():

  • Option A (recommended first test): a 24-hour "welcome offer" — the discounted annual SKU you already have configured (1YR29/1YR19 exist in App Store Connect) with honest framing: "Before you go — first year {price}, this week only." Use a real expiry, not a fake timer.
  • Option B: offer the weekly plan as a "just try it" downsell.
  • Also wire a winback: if a user cancels during trial, surface a 30–50% annual offer at next session (Airbridge: minutes-level intent window), and configure Apple win-back offers in App Store Connect for lapsed subs (free distribution).

Keep PWYC as-is — it already covers the affordability segment; the decline path covers the hesitation segment. Don't chain offers (Guideline 5.6).

Expected impact: +10–15% ARPU (Superwall/Adapty consensus)

Effort: 2–4 days.

P1 5.4 Unify and strengthen social proof

  • Fix the number contradiction: the quiz-unsegmented subhead says "500,000+ believers" while benefit rows say "100,000 ground-takers" — both can render on the same screen. Pick one true number and use it everywhere (a single constant).
  • Move the star banner + featured testimonial above the benefits so trust context precedes price (Airbridge: frequent measurable winner). Consider adding one "as seen in" / category-rank laurel if you have one.
  • Keep testimonials in testimony register ("God used this app to…") — it's the niche's native proof format.

Expected impact: modest lift + removes a credibility self-own

Effort: hours.

P1 5.5 Fix the SurveyPersonalizationEngine benefit copy

The 4 value-prop titles are near-identical long sentences across all 7 goal words (props 2–4 literally identical), titles/descriptions read swapped, and ctaText/urgencyText/challengeName are never rendered (dead code). Rewrite as: short benefit title (3–6 words, goal-specific) + one-line description. Copy-only tests have the lowest win rate (34.6%) so don't over-invest — but broken templating is below baseline, not a test.

Effort: half a day.

P2 5.6 Structural pricing tests (the highest win-rate test category, 59.6%)

  1. Weekly + 3-day trial vs annual-led: you already have the useWeeklyPlan flag and SpeakLife1Wk5. Adapty 2026: weekly+3-day-trial is the single best 1-yr-LTV config (1.5x average); weekly is now 55.6% of industry revenue. Caveat: faith/H&F skews annual (Hallow is annual-led) — so test, don't assume.
  2. Trial on annual only (no trial on the short plan) to push the high-LTV SKU — emerging 2026 standard.
  3. Trial length 3 vs 7 days: 5–9-day trials convert 37–45% vs 25–30% for ≤4 days. You already own 7-day-trial SKUs (1YR49, 1YR39) — this is a Remote Config flip once the hardcoded "3 days" string is fixed (5.1).
  4. Lifetime as a third option or inside PWYC: older Christian users distrust auto-renew; lifetime ($99.99 exists) converts the subscription-averse (Hallow sells $149.99 lifetime).
  5. Localize prices/strings — localization is the #1 win-rate test type (62.3%). Your "$%.2f" formatting and "$30/mo" footnote are USD-hardcoded today.

P2 5.7 Smaller paywall items

  • Keep the 4-second delayed X on soft surfaces — research says don't hide it further; the dismissal event (now monetized via 5.3) is worth more.
  • Test an animated hero (subtle motion on starrySunrise, button pulse): +8–15% documented for production-quality motion; skip cheap video.
  • Re-enable the commented-out closing line ("God prepared the treasure chest…") under the CTA — warmth above the legal links costs nothing.
  • The comparison grid is good and rare in the niche; keep "Other apps" unnamed (your code comment is right about the legal risk).

6. Warfare & Product Onboarding — What I Would Change

P0 6.1 Deepen the quiz from 1 question to 6–8

Keep the narrative screens — they're your emotional differentiator — but convert investment-free tap-throughs into investment. After the burden picker, add 4–6 quick single-tap questions that visibly feed the plan:

  1. "How long has this battle been going on?" (weeks / months / years / as long as I can remember) — agitation + sunk cost.
  2. "What have you already tried?" (prayer alone / devotionals / therapy / pushing through / all of it) — primes "this is different," mirrors your Priya K. testimonial.
  3. "When does it hit hardest?" (3am wake-ups / mornings / under pressure / at night) — feeds notification timing AND paywall copy ("before the next 3am wake-up" already exists in your quiz-segment copy — earn it here).
  4. "How do you connect best with the Word?" (speaking out loud / listening / reading / journaling) — feature-routing question, sells breadth.
  5. "How much time can you give this daily?" (1 min / 3 min / 10 min) — every answer is a yes; classic micro-commitment.
  6. Micro-insight interstitial after Q2–Q3 (the FunnelFox pattern): e.g. "73% of believers say anxious thoughts return within an hour of praying. Speaking Scripture out loud is how you hold the ground." — only use stats you can stand behind.

Each answer should echo somewhere downstream (plan reveal, paywall subhead, notification copy). The data is unambiguous: this is the single biggest onboarding lever you have.

Expected impact: double-digit trial-start lift (Lose It!, Adapty cases)

Effort: ~1 week incl. copy + plumbing answers into SurveyResponses.

P0 6.2 Add the plan-builder loader + named plan reveal before the paywall

Between the rating screen and the paywall (both flows), insert:

  1. Loader (3–5s): "Building your battle plan…" with sequential checkmarks: "Matching declarations to your fight ✓ · Choosing your verses ✓ · Setting your daily rhythm ✓ · Trusted by 100,000+ believers ✓" (social proof inside the loader is the 2026 standard).
  2. Plan reveal: a named plan — "Your 30-Day Take-Back-My-Peace Plan" — showing their burden, their personal declaration (you already have it!), their chosen time, and a realistic arc ("Week 1: quiet the 3am thoughts · Week 2: first-response Scripture · Week 4: standing, not spiraling"). CTA: "Unlock My Plan →" straight into the paywall.

This converts the paywall from "subscribe to an app" into "unlock the plan that's already mine" — the Noom/Bible Chat mechanic, and it composts perfectly with your existing personal-declaration headline.

Expected impact: +10–15% (FunnelFox loader data) plus paywall-personalization compounding

Effort: 3–5 days.

P1 6.3 Make the personal-declaration step skippable — carefully

It's your best moment, but it's mandatory voice/text input with no exit (and a save failure dead-ends with "try again"). Add a quiet "Skip for now" after ~6 seconds or after a failed attempt, defaulting the user to their burden's preview declaration so the paywall continuity copy still works. Instrument entry/complete/skip (to PostHog — see §7) and then decide: if completion is >85%, keep it mandatory; if it's leaking 20%+, the skip pays for itself.

Effort: 1 day.

P1 6.4 Standardize the pre-paywall sequence across arms

Warfare inserts the product-recap screen between rating and paywall; product puts it mid-flow. That's a hidden confound in your A/B readout (warfare's rating ask sits one screen further from the paywall). Pick one position — I'd put the recap mid-flow in both (product's placement) and let the new plan reveal (6.2) be the universal pre-paywall screen — so the arms differ only in their narrative front half and the comparison is clean.

Effort: hours.

P2 6.5 Test an opening testimonial wall

Bible Chat (fastest-growing faith app) opens with a scrolling wall of testimonies before the quiz — trust before ask. You have 5 paywall testimonials; surface 3 of them as a swipe-through screen right after the hook. Faith audiences run on testimony; front-loading it also softens the "is this another cash-grab app" reflex documented in Hallow's backlash.

Effort: 1–2 days.

P2 6.6 Keep (don't move) the notification flow

Time-picker → mock push preview → OS prompt as the terminal step is correct: post-paywall placement avoids delaying the money screen, and the preview-of-their-own-declaration is a strong primer. One addition: if permission is granted and the user started a trial, schedule the trial-ending local reminder here (pairs with 5.1's timeline promise).

8. Faith-Niche Strategic Plays

"Users generally expect faith-based services to be free… premium must be positioned as enhancement, never gatekeeping the essentials." — Ryan Beck, CTO, Pray.com (Sub Club podcast)
  1. Add a generosity layer to the paywall. The three faith leaders all monetize through generosity framing: Glorify's pay-it-forward sponsor tiers, Hallow's one-for-one donated subscription, Bible Chat's post-purchase charity reframe. You're one line away: under the CTA — "Every subscription gives SpeakLife free to a believer who can't afford it." (Your PWYC program literally makes this true.) Then add a post-purchase screen that reframes the buy as mission: "You just helped someone speak life over their storm." This lifts conversion and inoculates against the "profiting off faith" backlash documented against Hallow.
  2. Run the liturgical calendar as your promo calendar. Hallow's single biggest funnel is Pray40 (Lent) / Pray25 (Advent) — the challenge requires a trial, so the challenge IS the trial-start mechanism, with culturally legitimate urgency ("starts Ash Wednesday") replacing fake timers. A "40-Day Speak Life Challenge" is fully copyable at your scale, and your survey engine already has dead challengeName plumbing waiting for it.
  3. Keep the daily declaration genuinely free. The documented backlash trigger in this niche isn't price — it's gating "prayer itself" and surprise charges. Your hard onboarding paywall is fine (Hallow gates 85%), but make sure one daily declaration always works free, and over-communicate trial renewal (the local reminder from 5.1). In this audience a surprise charge isn't churn, it's a public testimony against you.
  4. Widget + multiple daily declaration moments. The "I am" app's growth engine ($300–600K/mo) is the lock-screen widget — declarations surfacing ambiently all day. Same mechanic, scriptural content; nobody at scale has combined them. Frame streaks as faithfulness ("days speaking God's Word") the way YouVersion and Hallow do.
  5. Gift & group flows later: gift subscriptions, a family plan (~1.7x annual), and church/group codes (Hallow's parish model scaled down) match how faith spreads — household and congregation, not individual.

9. Prioritized Roadmap

Week 1 — Fix the measurement (no experiments are valid until this ships)

  • Route all per-step onboarding + paywall events through AnalyticsService.track (PostHog included); tag shared back-half events with flow; set segment/burden for all arms. (§7 #1–2)
  • Fix trial_started gating/duplication, add weekly to PlanType, read the real intro-offer period, fix restore() and the social-proof number. (§7 #3–5, 7–8)

Weeks 2–3 — The three proven paywall/flow upgrades

  • Trial-timeline section + reassurance stack + local trial-ending reminder (5.1, 5.2) — +15–23% trials expected
  • Plan-builder loader + named plan reveal before the paywall, both arms, standardized sequence (6.2, 6.4) — +10–15%
  • Decline path: one-step welcome offer on dismissal + trial-cancel winback (5.3) — +10–15% ARPU

Weeks 4–6 — The big onboarding lever

  • Extend both arms' quizzes to 6–8 questions with one micro-insight interstitial, answers flowing into the plan reveal and paywall copy (6.1) — double-digit trial-start lift expected
  • Personal-declaration skip + instrumentation (6.3); survey-engine copy fix (5.5); generosity line + post-purchase mission screen (§8.1)

Ongoing — Structural experiment queue (highest win-rate categories first)

  1. Trial length: 3-day vs 7-day annual SKU (you already own both products — RC flip after 5.1)
  2. Weekly+3-day-trial vs annual-led (flag exists; analytics fix #4 is the prerequisite)
  3. Trial on annual only
  4. Price localization
  5. Opening testimonial wall (6.5); lifetime as third option; animated hero
  6. Plan a 40-day challenge for Advent 2026 (build starts ~September)

Target cadence: 2–3 experiments/month. Cal AI's 3x revenue came from ~5/month for 10 months; Adapty's data says ~15 experiments/year separates the 40x apps from the rest. Judge experiments on realized trial-to-paid and Day-0 cancellation (55% of 3-day-trial cancels are Day-0 buyer's remorse — your honesty metric), not trial starts alone.
