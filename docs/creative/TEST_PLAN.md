# Persona × Formula Test Plan (Oct–Nov 2026)

Goal: find which **personas** convert and which **Copy Vault formulas** convert, without mixing the
two up. Each round changes one thing: hold the persona fixed and sweep formulas, then hold the
formula fixed and sweep personas. Built on the Faith-Scale 2-campaign system and the creative-engine
loop. Track every ad in `SpeakLife_Ad_Tracker.xlsx` and every lesson in `LEARNINGS.md`.

## Structure

| Campaign | What runs | Budget |
|---|---|---|
| **SL Scale – Mind winners** (live) | The 4 proven/baseline "mind" ads (2 carousels, V1, V2). The control everything is compared to. | $100/day (women 25-65) |
| **SL Test – Persona × Formula** (new) | One new ad set a week, 5 ads each, ABO, $50/day per ad set, at most 2 ad sets live at once. | $50–100/day |

Test ad sets: same setup as the scale set (app promotion, trial-start optimization, US+CA, iOS 17+,
not installed, women 25-65 to match the baseline), exclude existing app users, Advantage+ creative
off, one primary text and one headline per ad. A week at $50/day is about $70 per ad, roughly 2×
the $30 target, which is the minimum to judge an ad.

## Production per round

- 5 ads, built mostly as **native slideshows or statics**: they're our cheapest format ($24 per
  trial) and fast to make with the existing slideshow and static-ad skills.
- **Video only for winners.** Once an ad wins, rebuild it as a Higgsfield video (about 60 credits)
  following the V1/V2 recipe in the creative-engine skill.
- Every ad: persona moment in line 1, serve-first unless the formula is a sell formula, verse quoted
  accurately, `ob=` link matching the persona, no Personal Attributes phrasing.
- Name ads `F{n}-{formula}-{persona}-{L|S}-{ob}`.

## Decision rules (day 7, extend to day 10 if spend is short)

- **Ad winner:** cost per trial ≤ $30 after ≥ $60 spend → breed one variation into the next round and
  load the original into the Scale campaign by post ID.
- **Watch:** $30–45 → leave running a few more days.
- **Kill:** > $45, or $60 spent with 0 trials.
- **Formula ranking:** by best and blended cost per trial in the tracker's Formulas tab. Any formula
  with a winner earns a persona sweep.
- **Persona advances** if it beats the mind baseline (~$32 blended). Personas that win get their own
  ad set in the Scale campaign.
- Judge on cost per trial, never CTR (Battlefield V2: 14.7% CTR, $43 per trial).

## Timeline for the 4 Scale ads (Day 0 = the day they're switched on)

Sources: Faith-Scale (run 7–14 days without touching; check winners around day 5; breed at day 7;
verdict at day 14; scale campaigns need 3–5 days because they mix warm and cold; never raise budget
more than 20% per 48 hours); Copy Vault FAQ (don't kill an ad until it has spent 2× the price).
Our version of "2× the price" is 2× the $30 target cost per trial, so $60.

| Day | What to do |
|---|---|
| 0–3 | Hands off. No edits, no budget changes: every edit restarts Meta's learning. |
| 3–5 | First read. Pause any single ad that has spent $60 with 0 trials. If the ad set is at or under $30 per trial, raise the budget 20% ($100 → $120). |
| 5–7 | If cost per trial is still healthy, another +20% (no more than once every 48 hours). |
| 7 | Verdict per ad: Winner ≤ $30 · Watch $30–45 · Kill > $45 (only once it has spent $60). If V1 or V2 spent under $30 (Meta favoring the proven carousels), don't kill it: move it into the Test campaign so it gets a fair run. |
| 14 | Final call on every "Watch" ad: off unless it has come down to $30 or less. |
| Weekly after | Winners wear out faster the more you spend. Turn off anything that drifts above $45 after 2× spend, and keep feeding new winners in from the Test campaign. |

If switched on Oct 1: first read Oct 4–6, verdict Oct 8, final call Oct 15.

## Caveats

- Meta won't spend evenly across the 5 ads. It backs its favorites early, and that's a signal in
  itself, but a starved ad hasn't really been tested. Re-run anything that spent under $30.
- Persona tests measure ad + onboarding together (each `ob=` arm converts differently). That's the
  number that matters, but say so when reading results.
- Women 25-65 only for now, to match the baseline. Test broad after week 4.
