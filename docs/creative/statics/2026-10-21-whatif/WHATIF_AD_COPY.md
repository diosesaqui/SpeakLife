Breeds **PAIN-06 fever what-if**, the account's cheapest trial over the last 14 days ($817 spend, 75
trials, **$10.90 per trial**). Each ad keeps the mechanic: name the what-if, then give the verse to
say back to it. Three things change on every ad, so the test can tell us which one is working:

## Checks done before handoff

- Every verse is NIV and matches the image word for word: Ps 112:7, Ps 107:20, Ps 4:8, Jer 30:17,
  Luke 4:39, Phil 4:19, Ps 37:25, Ps 121:8, Isa 26:3, Isa 46:4.
- Personal Attributes: no line says the viewer has an illness, a diagnosis, debt or anxiety. Openers
  name a **moment or role** ("To anyone refreshing the patient portal", "Parents up at 2am"). 02 and 05
  are the closest to the line ("When the pain shows up at night", "The bills are on the table"). If
  either gets rejected, change it to "For the nights pain keeps someone awake" or "For the nights the
  bills are on the table".
- No outcome claims: every ad promises a verse to speak, never a healed body, a kept job or a paid bill.
