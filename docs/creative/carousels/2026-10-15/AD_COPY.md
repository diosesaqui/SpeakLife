## The DNA these copy from

Two best ads in the account (main account `253451158661796`, read 2026-10-15):

| Ad | Format | Spend | Trials | Cost per trial |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **PAIN-06 fever what-if (sickness)** | static + long copy | $385 | 45 | **$8.56** |
| **you cant fight a thought silently** (active run) | 7-card carousel | $1,098 | 73 | **$15.04** |

What both do, and every deck below now does:

1. **The hook promises a method and teases one step:** "how to fight anxious thoughts with
   scripture (step 2 is the key)". The tease is what earns the swipe.
2. **"Jesus didn't pray about it. He spoke to it."** The mechanism in one contrast, named
   in the first seconds.
3. **Straight into numbered steps.** No scene-setting slides before them. The carousels
   lost their two "most nights end the same way" slides for this reason.
4. **SpeakLife appears exactly once, on the step called "the key"**, as the tool that makes the
   hard step easy ("type the thought, it hands you the verse", "a thought fought silently
   wins. SpeakLife keeps the declarations one tap away"). Every other step is free advice.
5. **The close is "X doesn't get the last word. His does."**
6. **Carousel post text is one line plus "step 2 is the whole key. save this."** The long
   step-by-step copy belongs on single-image ads (PAIN-06).

Link every ad to `ob=promises`. Judge on cost per trial. The 2x ROAS cap is about $9.

## Notes

- **Guardrails:** neither ad asks about the viewer's condition (Meta Personal Attributes). The
  body what-if closes on "keep the appointment. He's in that room too" so it never reads as
  skip the doctor. The money what-if promises the Father's care, never a dollar amount.
- **Small Scripture quirk:** the slideshow renderer capitalises "His Word" automatically, so
  Psalm 107:20 reads "He sent out His Word" on slide 5 of the body deck. NIV prints it
  lowercase. It's a capitalisation-only difference.
- **"7 days free"** on the close slides matches the hard onboarding wall. The winning ads said
  "free"; the download is free, the app needs the trial.
- **Re-render:** `python3 docs/creative/carousels/render_jesus_routines.py` and
  `python3 docs/creative/slideshows/render_jesus_routines.py`
