# Promise Angles Bank

Every angle runs on the same idea as the Promises onboarding: **God already made a promise about
this area of your life. It has never failed. The only question is whether you'll trust it and speak
it over your own life.** Each area is a door, and the promise is where it leads.

**Ad spine for every angle (serve-first):**
1. **The moment** (persona, plain words, never "Are you struggling with…")
2. **The promise** (one verse, NIV, quoted exactly)
3. **The method** (say it out loud, over that exact area)
4. **The bridge** ("want a promise ready for this every morning? that's what SpeakLife does")

**Rules:** never promise another free person will change (marriage, prodigal, children); never
promise an outcome the verse doesn't make; name the low moment only as the setting, then call
higher (CLAUDE.md Rule 12); no Meta Personal Attributes phrasing (no "you" + health, money or
mental-health state).

Priority = data fit (the account converts best with women 45+ on the mind angle) plus how strongly
Scripture speaks to the area. `ob=` is the deep link that lands the ad in the matching onboarding.

## How these feed the test plan

- **Week 3–4 persona sweeps become promise sweeps:** hold the winning formula from weeks 1–2 and
  run 5 promise angles per week. Order: Health, Provision, Protection, Sleep, Growing older, then
  Children/grandkids, Fear, Strength, Grief, Not alone.
- Each winning angle gets the full treatment: a native slideshow, then a video (V1/V2 recipe), then
  Promises vs Storm onboarding.
- Log every shipped angle in the tracker's Angle column so the Angles tab scores it.
