## Health guardrails (non-negotiable)

- **Topic, not the reader.** Write "healing promises to speak over aching joints," never "Do you
  have arthritis?" or "Your blood pressure…". No "you" + a condition anywhere, including headlines.
- **No cure or outcome claims** for the app or the declarations ("lowers blood pressure", "heals
  cancer", "pain free in 7 days"). Scripture makes the promise, quoted exactly with its reference;
  SpeakLife is where you speak it.
- **Never replace care.** Nothing that tells anyone to stop treatment or medication. Speaking the
  Word sits alongside the doctor, not against it ("the drive to the appointment", "the waiting
  room", "before the scan").
- **Call higher** (CLAUDE.md Rule 12): the symptom is only the setting; the line lands on the promise.
- App claims verified in `storm_configs.json` (health): "a healing declaration ready for every
  appointment and every symptom", nightly audio of God's promises, the 7-day Health plan.
