**Category:** Fear. Every ad names one fear only as the setting, then lands on God's promise for it
and the act of saying it out loud. Same 5 formulas in every ad set, so results read two ways: which
**fear** converts and which **formula** converts. $50/day per ad set, women 25-65, US+CA, iOS 17+,
trial-start optimization.

**Guardrails:** the fear is the topic, never a label on the reader (no "Afraid of losing your job?",
"Worried about your health?"). No outcome claims (no "you won't get laid off", "nothing bad will
happen"); Scripture makes the promise, quoted exactly. Call higher: name the fear once, then let
every following line stand on the promise. FE1 avoids financial-status language (no "broke",
"in debt"). FE2 never discourages seeing a doctor.
