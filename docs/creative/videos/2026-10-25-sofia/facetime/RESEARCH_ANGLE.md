# Angle research — 2026-10-25

Meta Ad Library, US, active and recent ads. The library returns titles and dates only (no body
copy or spend), so volume and repetition are the signal: an advertiser running dozens of variants
of one title, or a second page copying it, is paying for what works.

## What competitors are betting on

**Faithful Together** copies "Feel Seen Again", so that one is likely a proven title.
**Mason Cain** (men): shame cycle, "It's not a spiritual failure. It's a nervous system response."
**Amanda Johnson**: "Why women are walking away from their faith."
**Hallow, Glorify, Creed, Bible Nova**: generic offers ("Try Hallow for Free", "Pray Without
Ceasing", "Bible Study for Every Mood"). No sharp pain.

## Our own data (LEARNINGS.md)
- Thoughts are our cheapest storm; "you can't fight a thought silently" is our best line.
- Abstract identity hooks lost ("new identity in Christ" $217 per trial). Identity works as payoff.
- Condemnation as such is untested here (PAIN-07 "the replay" got $0 spend).

## Decision
**Worth, told through the comparison voice:** the "not enough" thought after scrolling at night.
It is the pain competitors spend most on, it is a *thought* (our best storm), and it fits a woman
in her 20s exactly. Mechanism and payoff stay ours: answer the voice out loud with what God said.
