by `caption_v2.py` rather than spoken.

**On-screen hook (0-3s):** prayer asks. declaration enforces.

> Prayer and declaration aren't the same thing.
> Prayer asks God for what you don't have yet.
> A declaration enforces what already belongs to you.
> Your health. "By His wounds we are healed."
> Your peace. "My peace I give you."
> Your provision. "My God will meet all your needs."
> Jesus didn't pray about the storm. He spoke to it.
> So keep praying. But what He already gave you, speak it.
> SpeakLife puts His promises in your mouth every morning.
