# The 15 Formulas, adapted for SpeakLife

Each entry: what it does, the steps (kept from the Copy Vault), the SpeakLife adaptation, and one
example. Examples are illustrations of structure. Before using a verse, check it against the
onboarding arm the ad links to (`Views/Onboarding/Model/OnboardingAngles.swift`) so ad and app say
the same thing. Bracketed proof (`[rating]`) must be filled with real numbers or cut.

---

## 1. The Revival Reel — dream → truth → path
Paints the dream, bridges to reality, hands them a path.

1. Callout (moment/role, not attribute) 2. "Imagine…" vivid ideal 3. "The truth is…" it's possible
4. "You can…" 2–3 things 5. Introduce product 6. 3 benefit bullets (action verbs)
7. "This isn't just… it's…" 8. CTA

**Adaptation:** the dream is the *higher reality* the verse promises (Rule 12), not the absence of
the problem. Best fed into **Promises**.

> For the mornings you wake up already bracing.
>
> Imagine starting the day with His peace settled in your mind before your phone gets a word in.
>
> The truth is, that peace was left to you on purpose. *"Peace I leave with you; my peace I give
> you."* John 14:27
>
> You can speak it over your mind every morning, out loud, in 60 seconds.
>
> SpeakLife gives you:
> ✓ Declarations rooted in a verse, ready to speak
> ✓ A plan built around the one area weighing on you
> ✓ Audio to fall asleep to His promises
>
> This isn't another devotional to read. It's His Word in your own mouth.
>
> Start tomorrow's morning tonight 👇

## 2. The Third Door — two tried options, then the one they didn't see
1. The core question 2. "You could…" obvious fix 3. "You could…" second attempt
4. "Or… you could…" product 5. Mechanism 6. 3 "beneath the surface" bullets
7. Emotional payoff line 8. Offer + CTA

**Adaptation:** Door 1 and 2 are things believers genuinely do (pray harder, read more, worry it
through). Never mock them. Door 3 is speaking, the way Jesus did. This is SpeakLife's natural
formula; the mechanism is the six-beat spine compressed.

> What do you do with a promise while you're still waiting on it?
>
> You could pray about it again tonight.
> You could read another chapter and hope it sinks in.
>
> Or… you could do what Jesus did. He didn't ask the storm to calm down. He spoke to it.
>
> SpeakLife puts a declaration rooted in Scripture in your mouth every morning, over the exact
> area you're standing on.
>
> • You stop rehearsing the problem.
> • You start saying what God already said.
> • Your heart catches up to what's already yours.
>
> And one morning you notice you're not bracing anymore.
>
> Try it free 👇
